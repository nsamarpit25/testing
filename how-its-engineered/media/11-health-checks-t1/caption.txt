3 AM. Your app is still running, but it stopped answering anyone. Nothing crashed, so nothing restarts it. 🥶

Running isn't the same as working. So something has to keep asking "are you okay?", like a nurse checking a pulse:

💓 Liveness: every few seconds it hits your health page. Three misses in a row and the app gets restarted
🚦 Readiness: a copy that's still starting says "not yet", and traffic skips it until it's ready

In Kubernetes that's a few lines of config.

Has your app ever been "up" but not working? 👇
