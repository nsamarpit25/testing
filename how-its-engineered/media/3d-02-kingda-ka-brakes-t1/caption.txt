0 to 128 mph in 3.5 seconds. Now try stopping it, with no brake pads touching anything. 🧲

Kingda Ka's train carries strong magnets past copper fins. The moving magnets push electric currents through the copper, and those currents push back, slowing the train smoothly. Faster train, stronger braking, and no power needed.
