Your chat app is on a phone call with a server that never hangs up. That's why messages show up before you refresh.

📞 Normal requests: your app asks, the server answers once, and the line closes
🔌 A WebSocket keeps one connection open, so either side can talk any time
⚡ When your friend hits send, the server pushes it straight down that line to you
🧠 The catch: every open line costs server memory, and dropped connections need a reconnect

Discord uses WebSockets for live updates, and every modern browser supports them.

Would your app notice if its connection quietly dropped?
