In 2010, people in South Korea were reported unlocking iPhones with snack sausages. 🌭

It was winter, and their phones ignored gloved fingers. Under the glass is a grid of see-through wires carrying a tiny electric signal. Your finger, mostly salty water, pulls away part of that signal where it touches. Wool barely changes it. A salty, moist sausage does.

Your screen never knew it was your finger.

What's the weirdest thing you've used to tap your phone? 👇
