Nine seconds to load ten orders, because the app pulled a million rows and kept ten.

📊 The database holds every order from every user, with a column for each detail
🚚 "Send me everything" moves a million rows to your app, which throws most away
🎯 A better query asks for exactly what's on screen: your 10 newest, only the columns shown
🔍 Put EXPLAIN in front of a query to see if it scans the whole table

Fetching less is often the cheapest speed-up your app will ever get.
