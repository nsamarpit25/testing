Your app went viral and your database is crashing. The twist: almost all that traffic is just reading.

📖 Feeds, profiles, search = reads. Posting = a write.
🪞 Read replicas are copies that get every change from the primary
✍️ Writes still go to the primary; reads spread across the copies
⏳ Copies can lag a moment behind (replication lag)
✅ Fix: right after you write, read your own data from the primary for a few seconds

Replicas scale reads, not writes.
