Black Friday. Your store is down, and nothing is even broken. The reviews just got slow.

🐢 Every product page waits on the reviews server
📈 The waiting work piles up until the whole store stops loading
⚡ A circuit breaker sees too many slow or failed requests and pauses them
🧩 Your code skips the reviews and loads the rest of the page
🔁 Later it lets a few test requests through. Fast again? Resume.

One slow feature shouldn't take down your whole app.

Which service in your app would take everything else down with it?
