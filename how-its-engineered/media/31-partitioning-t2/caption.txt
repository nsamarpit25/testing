A billion rows, and last week's orders still load fast. The trick is that the database never looks at most of them.

🗂️ Partitioning splits one huge table into smaller ones inside the same database
📅 A partition key, usually a date, decides which partition each row goes into
⏭️ Ask for last week, and the database skips every month that can't hold it
🧹 Old data? Drop the oldest partition in one step instead of deleting millions of rows

Not the same as sharding: partitions stay in one database, shards spread across many servers.
