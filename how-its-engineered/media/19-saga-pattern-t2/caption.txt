You charged for the flight and the hotel. Then the car booking fails. Who refunds the money?

🧩 Each booking lives in a separate program, with no shared undo button
📋 A saga runs the steps one by one, each with a cleanup plan
↩️ When the car fails, it cleans up backwards: cancel the hotel, then the flight, then refund
🧳 Like a travel agent undoing your trip in reverse

The cleanup steps are called compensating actions, and you write them. AWS Step Functions can run the whole saga.

Does your checkout undo the first charge when step 2 fails?
