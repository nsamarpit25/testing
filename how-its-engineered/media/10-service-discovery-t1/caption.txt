Same code. New address. And every call to your service failed. 💥

The address was hardcoded. In the cloud, servers restart and move all the time, so the number you saved goes stale.

Save the name, not the number, like your phone contacts:
📝 a starting service registers "I'm payments, here's my address"
🔍 callers look up payments before every call
💓 the registry pings every copy and drops the ones that stop answering

Kubernetes does this for you: call it by name.
