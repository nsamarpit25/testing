The gas around these blades is hotter than the metal's melting point. So why don't they melt?

Three tricks: each blade is grown as one single crystal of metal, air pumps through tiny cooling holes, and a ceramic coat shields the surface. Together they keep a blade alive at thousands of RPM. 🔥
