The server went quiet for 30 seconds on purpose, and that's what made your chat feel instant.

🔁 Plain polling: ask every few seconds, hear "nothing new" almost every time
⏳ Long polling: the server holds your request open and waits
📬 The moment a message lands, it answers, and your app asks again
⏱️ No message before the time limit? It answers "nothing yet" and the loop restarts

Telegram's Bot API offers long polling (getUpdates) over plain HTTPS.

Did you know there was a real-time trick older than WebSockets?
