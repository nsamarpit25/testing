Your checkout queue is stuck on one broken order. The app keeps retrying it, and everything behind it piles up.

☠️ A message that fails every time is a "poison message"
🔢 Set a retry limit
📦 After that, it's moved aside into a dead letter queue
✅ Every other order keeps flowing
🔧 Fix the cause later and send it back to be processed

Amazon SQS and RabbitMQ both support it.

Have you ever had one bad message block a whole queue?
