Your orders page makes 101 trips to the database. It only needed two. 🐢

One query fetches 100 orders. Then your code asks for each order's customer, one at a time: 100 more trips, each one waiting on the last. That's the N+1 problem.

Fix: fetch all the customers at once with WHERE id IN (…), or turn on eager loading in your ORM.
