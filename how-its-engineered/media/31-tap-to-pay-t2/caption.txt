Before a card machine can take your money, it has to power your card. ⚡

Your bank card has no battery. The reader makes a magnetic field that cycles 13.56 million times a second, and a coil hidden around the card's edge turns it into just enough electricity to wake the chip. The chip answers with your card number plus a one-time code that only works for that payment.

On the London Tube, the gate checks the card is genuine on the spot and the bank settles up later, so nobody waits.

Did you know your card was just plastic until you tap? 👇
