One update blue-screened 8.5 million computers. All at the same time 💥

Airports, banks, hospitals. Down.

Coal miners had a fix: send a canary down first 🐤 If the bird stops singing, everyone gets out.

A canary release does the same for code:
🐤 Ship the new version to 1% first
👀 Watch for crashes, errors, reboots
↩️ Something spikes? Roll back automatically
📈 All good? 5%, then 25%, then everyone

That's Argo Rollouts and AWS CodeDeploy.

Does your team ship to everyone at once? 👇
