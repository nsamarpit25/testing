Saturday night, peak traffic, and your app freezes. The process says "running" but nobody gets an answer. 30 seconds later something restarts it. What noticed? 🤔

Running isn't working. A frozen app looks alive, so crash recovery never fires. The fix is a health check: the system hosting your app keeps asking "are you okay?"

✅ Are you alive? Hit a health page every few seconds. 3 missed answers in a row → restart
⏳ Are you ready? A new copy says "not yet" while it starts, so requests skip it

Kubernetes calls these liveness and readiness probes.

Which one have you actually configured? 👇
