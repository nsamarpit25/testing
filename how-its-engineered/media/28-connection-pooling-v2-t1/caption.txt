8 PM, peak traffic. Your app is throwing errors, but the database is running fine. It just won't take another connection. 🚕

Every request was opening its own database connection, and Postgres only allows so many. A connection pool opens a few connections once, keeps them open, and lends them out: a request borrows one, uses it, hands it back.

Like a taxi rank: a few cabs serve the whole queue.

How big is your app's pool? 👇
