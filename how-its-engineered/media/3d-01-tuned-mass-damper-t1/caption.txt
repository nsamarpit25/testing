There's a 660-tonne steel ball hanging inside Taipei 101. In a typhoon, it swings, and that's what keeps the tower steady. 🌀

Wind makes a skyscraper sway. The ball, a tuned mass damper, swings the opposite way and soaks up that motion. In August 2015, a super typhoon swung it about a metre.

Would you want to see it move in person? 👇
