Your server went quiet for 1 second. Then thousands of apps retried at once and took it down for real. That's a retry storm.

1️⃣ Cap it: stop once it works, or after 3 retries
2️⃣ Exponential backoff: wait 1 s, then 2 s, then 4 s
3️⃣ Jitter: add a little randomness so apps don't all come back together
