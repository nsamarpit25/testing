In 2002 there was no Shazam app. You dialled 2580, held your phone up to the speaker, and got the song name back by text. 📟

The trick behind it hasn't changed. Shazam turns sound into a map of pitch over time, keeps only the brightest points, and links pairs of them into tiny codes. Those codes are a fingerprint of one exact recording, checked against millions of songs.

That's also why humming never works.

Did you ever use the 2580 number? 👇
