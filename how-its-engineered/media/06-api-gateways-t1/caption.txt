One service in a food app forgot to check the login. Suddenly anyone could see everyone's orders. 💥

When your phone calls every service directly, each one has to check your login itself. Miss one and the door is open.

An API gateway is the single front door, like airport check-in: it checks your login once, blocks someone spamming refresh, and routes one tap to all the services behind it. Kong and Amazon API Gateway are common ones.

Have you ever shipped an endpoint without an auth check? 👇
