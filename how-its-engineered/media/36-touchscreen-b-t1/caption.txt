Your phone ignores a wool glove, however hard you press. But in 2010, South Koreans were tapping iPhones with snack sausages.

Under the glass is an invisible grid of wires carrying a tiny electric signal. Your finger, mostly salty water, pulls away part of that signal where it touches. A glove barely changes it. A salty, moist sausage does.

Your screen never knew it was your finger. 🌭
