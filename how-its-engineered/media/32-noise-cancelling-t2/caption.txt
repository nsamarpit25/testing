Your headphones get quieter by adding sound, not removing it.

🎤 Microphones on each ear cup listen to the engine roar
🔄 A chip flips that sound wave upside down
🔊 The speaker plays the opposite wave, even with no music on
〰️ Where the two waves meet at your ear, much of the roar cancels out

It works best on steady low rumble. Voices change too fast, so you still hear people talking.

Did you know Bose started on this idea during a flight in 1978?
