You tapped Pay once and got charged twice. How does that even happen?

📶 The server charged you $50, but its reply got lost on bad Wi-Fi
🔁 Your app saw no reply and tried again → second charge
🏷️ The fix: your app labels each payment with a unique number and sends the same number on every retry
🗂️ The server saves the number with the result
✅ Seen it before? It sends back the first answer. No second charge.

That's idempotency. Stripe has it built in.
