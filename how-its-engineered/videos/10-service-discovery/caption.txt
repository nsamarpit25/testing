Your service restarted. Same code, new address. And every call to it failed. 💥

The bug: the address was written into the code. In the cloud, servers restart and move all the time, so that address goes stale.

The fix: save the name, not the number. Like your phone contacts. That's service discovery.

📝 Register: when a service starts, it tells a registry "I'm payments, here's my address"
🔍 Look up: before every call, the caller asks the registry where payments is right now
💓 Check: the registry keeps pinging every copy. One stops answering? It's off the list

In Kubernetes it's built in: call payments by name and it routes you to the right address.
