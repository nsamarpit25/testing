You hit post, and 2 million followers need a notification. So why is your post button still spinning? 📮

If your post service notifies followers, sends emails and updates feeds itself, one after another, one slow job freezes everything. And every new feature means rewriting it. Again.

Pub/Sub fixes it:
📣 The post service publishes ONE message to a topic, and it's done
📬 Every feature subscribes, and each gets its own copy at the same time
➕ New feature? Just subscribe it. The publisher never changes

📻 Like a radio station: it broadcasts once, and every radio tuned in hears it.

That's Google Cloud Pub/Sub and Amazon SNS.
