One slow service took down the whole app. Not broken. Just slow. ⚡

Every request waits on it, each one holds a thread, threads run out, and the server stops answering everyone. That's a cascading failure. Retries make it worse.

The fix is a circuit breaker, borrowed from your house:
🟢 Closed: calls go through and failures get counted
🔴 Open: too many failures, so calls fail instantly and the page hides that part
🟡 Half-open: after a cooldown, a few test calls decide whether it closes again

Netflix built Hystrix for this. Today Resilience4j does it in a few lines.
