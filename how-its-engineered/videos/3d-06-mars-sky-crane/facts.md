# Ep 6 facts (checked 2026-10-24)
Primary sources: NASA/JPL *MSL Landing Press Kit* (2012): https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/news/press_kits/MSLLanding.pdf (below: "MSL kit") and *Mars 2020 Landing Press Kit* (2021): https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/news/press_kits/mars_2020/download/mars_2020_landing_press_kit.pdf (below: "M2020 kit"). Cross-checked with Gemini Argon (qc/facts_reply.md) and web searches.

1. Line 1. **One-tonne rover.** Curiosity is 899 kg and Perseverance is 1,025 kg (MSL kit; M2020 kit). "One-tonne" is a fair rounding.
2. Line 2. **Too heavy for airbags.** Spirit and Opportunity (185 kg each) landed on airbags. NASA says airbags big enough for a rover of about a tonne would have been too heavy for the entry system to carry (MSL kit, EDL section).
3. Line 3. **Engines near the ground.** With thrusters firing close to the surface, a legged lander would raise a damaging dust cloud and dig pits under itself. The sky crane keeps the engines high above the rover (MSL kit; JPL "Seven Minutes of Terror", https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/videos/curiositys-seven-minutes-of-terror).
4. Line 4. **Twenty metres.** The sky-crane manoeuvre starts about 20 m above the surface (21.3 m in the M2020 timeline). The stage descends at a constant 0.75 m/s. It never stops: "slows to a crawl" is used instead of "hovers". The 0.75 M/S figure is on screen.
5. Line 4, visuals. **Eight engines, four used.** The stage has 8 throttleable hydrazine Mars Lander Engines. Four shut down as the sky crane starts, so the lowering runs on 4 (MSL kit p. 27–29; web search confirmed this for Curiosity). The film shows all 8 firing during braking, then 4 going out.
6. Line 5. **Three nylon cables.** Three nylon load-bearing bridles plus an electrical umbilical (drawn as a fourth, thinner grey line). The bridles are about 6.4 m long, and the stage-to-wheels distance at full stretch is 7.5 m (MSL kit; M2020 kit; planetary.org, https://planetary.org/space-images/curiositys-skycrane-maneuver). The narration gives no length. The on-screen "7.5 M" runs from the stage to the wheels.
7. Line 6. **Wheels as landing gear.** About 2 s after lowering starts, the mobility system (6 wheels on rocker-bogie suspension) deploys into landing configuration (MSL kit).
8. Line 7. **Blades slice the cables.** Touchdown is sensed from the drop in load (the stage's throttle response), not by wheel sensors. Pyrotechnic cutters then sever the bridles and umbilical. "Once its wheels touch down" is used rather than "the instant" (MSL kit).
9. Line 8. **Safe distance.** The requirement was at least 150 m. Curiosity's stage hit about 650 m away (PIA16002, https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/images/pia16002-curiositys-descent-stage-impact-site) and Perseverance's about 700 m away (PIA24333). The on-screen callout says "≈650 M AWAY" (Curiosity).
10. Line 9. **Both rovers.** Curiosity landed on 6 Aug 2012 (UTC) and Perseverance on 18 Feb 2021, both by sky crane (PIA24319, https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/images/pia24319-perseverance-touches-down-on-mars-illustration/).
11. Visual liberties. The scene is a generic Gale-crater-like plain and the rover geometry is simplified. Line 3's "what if" shot shows a hypothetical legged lander, not a real mission.
