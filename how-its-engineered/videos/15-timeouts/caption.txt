Your app asked a server for the weather, and nothing came back. How long should it wait? ⏳

By default, it might wait forever. Python's requests won't time out unless you pass a timeout, and Go's default http client is the same.

Waiting isn't free:
🧵 The slow call holds a thread while the spinner spins
📥 Every request stuck behind it holds another
🔴 Run out of threads and your app stops answering everyone

⏱️ A timeout is a deadline: the longest you'll wait before calling it a failure. timeout=3 means after 3 seconds you stop waiting.
✅ Then handle it: show a clear error, or fall back to the forecast you saved earlier.
