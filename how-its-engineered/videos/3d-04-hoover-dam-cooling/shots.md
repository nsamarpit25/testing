# Ep 4 shot plan: Hoover Dam's hidden fridge

~94 words, ~30 s at a fast read (~3.1 words/s). Durations are approximate; retime to the word timings after TTS.
Cuts every 1-2 s. No people, no vehicles with drivers, no fluid sims (water = shader planes).

## Look note
- Opening and closing: a realistic, atmospheric establishing shot like ep 3's airliner above clouds. Hoover Dam's
  curved arch-gravity face wedged in a dark, layered volcanic-rock canyon (displaced, noise-textured cliffs with
  strata, not low-poly), Lake Mead as a deep blue-green reflective shader plane with gentle normal-map ripples and
  the pale "bathtub ring" band on the rock, golden-hour sun low and raking across the dam face, warm haze/volumetric
  atmosphere, long soft shadows, a sky gradient. Intake towers as simple bevelled cylinders with domed caps.
- Mechanism shots: the "x-ray / thermal" language from ep 2-3 in a dark studio void. Concrete is a pale, slightly
  rough, bevelled material; thermal view swaps it for an emissive colour ramp (white-yellow > orange > red > deep
  violet > dark blue). Cooling pipes and water are the accent: glowing cyan emissive tubes with a flowing dash
  texture (animated mapping offset) to show flow. Grout is a warm grey that fills gaps with a wipe/mask.
- Premium: smooth subdivided, bevelled geometry, shallow depth of field, slow precise moves (push-in, orbit, rack
  focus) cut fast, bloom on emissive heat and cyan, light heat-haze shimmer (displacement noise on a refraction
  plane) over the hot pour. Optional tag overlays later: "125 YEARS", "582+ MILES", "1-INCH PIPE".
- Column geometry (accurate): trapezoidal columns rising in 5-ft lifts, about 60 ft square at the upstream face
  down to about 25 ft at the downstream face, with interlocking vertical grooves (keys) on the faces. Cooling coils
  are serpentine loops of thin pipe laid on each lift, feeding headers that run to the cliffs.

## Line 1 (~5.5 s): "A hundred and twenty-five years. That's how long Hoover Dam would take to cool in one pour."
1. Wide establishing: the dam in its canyon at golden hour, Lake Mead glinting behind; slow aerial push forward and
   slight descent over the lake towards the crest. (~2 s)
2. Low angle on the downstream face, sun raking across the concrete; push in towards the curved wall. (~1.5 s)
3. Macro push into the concrete surface (aggregate and form-line texture), then the surface dissolves into the
   thermal view: the whole dam as one solid mass glowing white-hot inside. (~2 s)

## Line 2 (~4.5 s): "Setting cement makes heat. The hot core fights the cooling skin, and it cracks."
1. Cutaway section through a single giant pour in the studio void: heat blooms out from the centre, colour ramp
   rising to white-orange, heat haze above. Slow orbit. (~1.5 s)
2. Close on the edge: the skin fades to blue while the core stays orange; a sharp temperature boundary. Push-in.
   (~1.5 s)
3. Cracks race across the surface (animated boolean/mask of a Voronoi crack pattern with glowing orange edges),
   small chunks drop; hard cut on "cracks". (~1.5 s)

## Line 3 (~2.3 s): "So it went up in separate columns."
1. Reset to clean concrete: the dam volume split into a grid of trapezoidal columns that rise in 5-ft lifts, staggered
   heights like a city skyline. Fast upward crane. (~1.2 s)
2. Close on two neighbouring columns: the interlocking grooves (keys) slide past each other. (~1.1 s)

## Line 4 (~3.9 s): "Inside each, coils of one-inch steel pipe. Over five hundred eighty miles."
1. X-ray one column: concrete goes translucent, revealing serpentine pipe coils on every lift; rack focus from the
   near coil to the stack behind. (~1.5 s)
2. Pull back fast and wide: every column x-rayed, the whole pipe network lighting up thin and dense like a circuit
   across the dam. (~2.4 s)

## Line 5 (~3.5 s): "River water first, then ice water from a giant refrigeration plant."
1. Flow arrows/dashes run through a coil in muted river green-blue; slow track along the pipe. (~1.2 s)
2. Cut to the refrigeration plant on the lower cofferdam: a stylised industrial block with bevelled tanks and
   compressor housings, frost shader on the pipes, cold breath (subtle volumetric fog); push in. (~1.2 s)
3. The pipe colour switches to bright cyan and the flow speeds up through the coil; hard match cut on "plant".
   (~1.1 s)

## Line 6 (~2.3 s): "As heat drains, the columns shrink apart."
1. Thermal view of the column grid: colour drains from orange to deep blue, starting next to each cyan coil and
   spreading outward. Slow orbit. (~1.2 s)
2. Close on a joint: the columns contract and a thin dark gap opens between them. Push-in. (~1.1 s)

## Line 7 (~3.2 s): "Grout fills the gaps and pipes, making one solid dam."
1. Grout (warm grey) wipes up the gap from the bottom, filling the grooves. (~1.2 s)
2. Inside a pipe: grout pushes through and the cyan glow is snuffed out, pipe ends cut off flush. (~1 s)
3. The column seams fade out until the block reads as one solid wall; slow pull back. (~1 s)

## Line 8 (~2.6 s): "Cooled in under two years, not a century."
1. Dissolve from the solid section back to the real dam face at golden hour, camera pulling back and up. (~1.3 s)
2. Wide reverse of the opening: the dam, canyon and Lake Mead with the sun touching the rim; slow pull out. (~1.3 s)

## Line 9 (~2.6 s): "Follow for how the world is really engineered."
1. Hold the wide shot, drift higher over the canyon; series end card / handle fades in. (~2.6 s)
