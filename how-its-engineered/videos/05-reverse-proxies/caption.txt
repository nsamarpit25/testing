Your phone thinks it's talking to Instagram's servers. It's not. 🤔

It's talking to a middleman: a reverse proxy.

Without one, every server sits on the open internet. Hackers only need to find one weak one.
With one, the servers hide behind a wall. Only the proxy has a public address.

And it does a lot more than guard the door:
🔒 Unlocks HTTPS once, so the servers don't have to
⚡ Keeps popular pages ready, so most requests never reach a server
🧭 Reads the path: /api goes here, images go there
🛡️ Stops floods of bad traffic at the door

Nginx and Cloudflare do this for millions of websites.
