You tapped Pay twice. So why were you only charged once? 🤔

Slow network → the reply gets lost → your app retries.
Without a safeguard, that's two charges.

The fix: idempotency.
Every payment gets a unique ID. The server checks: seen this ID before?
Yes → send back the same answer. No second charge.

Same request, many times. Same result, once.
