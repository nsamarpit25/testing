Your app froze at 3 AM. Still running, not answering anyone. And something restarted it before anyone woke up. 🥶

The trap: running isn't the same as working. A frozen app still looks alive, nothing crashed, so nothing restarts it.

The fix: keep asking it "are you okay?" Like a nurse checking your pulse. That's a health check.

💓 Alive? Every few seconds it calls your app's health page. Three misses in a row? Restart
🚦 Ready? A copy that's still starting up says "not yet", and traffic skips it until it is

In Kubernetes these are liveness and readiness probes: a few lines in your config.
