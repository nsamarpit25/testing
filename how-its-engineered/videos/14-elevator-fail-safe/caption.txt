If the ropes snap, what stops an elevator falling? 🛗

The car grabs the rails.

🪝 1854: Elisha Otis used the lifting rope to keep a spring bent. When the rope broke, the spring pushed catches into teeth on the rails. The failure itself fired the brake.
⚙️ Today: a thin governor rope turns a wheel. If the car goes too fast, weights swing out and lock that rope.
🔧 The car keeps moving, so the stopped rope pulls a lever, and wedges squeeze the rails.
💥 1945: debris cut an Empire State Building elevator's ropes, and its governor rope too. Car 6 fell 75 floors. Betty Lou Oliver survived.
📡 Newer systems drop that rope: a speed sensor fires the wedges directly.

Fail-safe: when something breaks, the system ends up safe.
