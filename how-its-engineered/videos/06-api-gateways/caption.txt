One service in your food app forgot to check the login. Now anyone can see everyone's orders. 💥

Your app isn't one program. It's small services: users, menu, payments, delivery. If your phone calls each one directly, every service has to check your login on its own. Miss one, and the door's wide open.

The fix? One front door. An API gateway. Like airport check-in: nobody boards without passing it.
🪪 Check ID: it checks your login once, then passes your ID along
🚦 Count: one user spams refresh? Blocked. Everyone else stays fast
🔀 Route: one tap on Order goes to 4 services, and it stitches the answers together

A gateway is a reverse proxy that understands your API.
Kong and Amazon API Gateway run it for real companies.
