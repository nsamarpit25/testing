Here is the technical and creative review of the 40-second Taipei 101 reel for `@how.its.engineered`.

### 1. Visual Glitches & Rendering Issues
*   **00:05.8 – 00:06.0 (Blown-out / Clipping):** As the camera zooms into the illuminated window on the tower, the exposure blows out completely to solid white/cream for ~0.2s before hard-cutting to the interior damper shot. It feels like an accidental camera clip through an emissive plane rather than an intentional flash transition.
*   **00:09.5 – 00:10.1 (Unreadable Geometry / Clipping):** During the match cut from the tower to the ruler, the camera starts *inside* or immediately flush against the white ruler geometry. For 0.6 seconds, it looks like a glitching white polygon cone before the camera pulls back to reveal the desk at **00:10.1**.
*   **00:12.4 – 00:13.3 & 00:35.5 – 00:37.4 ( Severe Depth-of-Field / Shader Artifacts):** In both close-ups of the water glass, the combination of the glass shader and heavy bokeh/DoF makes the cup look like a low-res, blurry smudge. The water surface barely reads as liquid.
*   **00:17.2 (3D Text Pop):** The "92F" floating text in the interior atrium pops on and off abruptly on a hard cut; it also lacks perspective integration.
*   **00:18.4 – 00:19.4 (Camera Clipping):** In the extreme close-up of the damper plates, the blue vertical suspension cables pass right through the camera's near-clipping plane, creating jarring dark blue vertical wipes across the frame.
*   **00:26.7 – 00:27.4 (Arrow Clipping):** The orange force arrow emerging from the damper clips awkwardly through the cyan wireframe struts of the building.
*   **00:33.2 – 00:35.2 (Mirrored / Upside-Down 3D Text):** The "1 m" floor measurement graphic under the damper is oriented upside-down/backwards relative to the camera's viewing angle, making it difficult to read quickly.

---

### 2. Visual vs. Narration Mismatches
*   **00:23.7 – 00:25.3 (Physics / Direction Mismatch):** The narrator says, *"so when wind shoves the tower one way, the heavy ball lags behind..."* Visually, the cyan wind streaks are moving **left-to-right**, which should shove the tower to the **right**. However, at **00:24.5**, the tower lurches to the **left** (into the wind).
*   **00:35.5 – 00:37.3 (Action Mismatch):** The narrator says, *"so the people inside didn't have to [move]."* Yet, in the close-up of the glass of water, the water is still sloshing back and forth almost as much as it did in the "seasick" shot at **00:12.5**. The water should be hampir completely still here to sell the damper's effectiveness.

---

### 3. Transitions Review
*   **What Works Well:**
    *   **00:01.7 & 00:06.4:** The lightning flashes acting as transition triggers/accents are punchy and fit the storm vibe well.
    *   **00:07.3 – 00:07.5:** The ball swinging directly into the lens to wipe to the exterior shot is a great 3D transition.
    *   **00:13.5 – 00:14.5:** The exterior skin dissolving into the cyan structural wireframe to reveal the glowing damper inside is clean and informative.
    *   **00:21.6 – 00:21.9 & 00:32.8 – 00:33.2:** The whip-pans with directional motion blur keep the energy high.
*   **What Feels Broken/Abrupt:**
    *   **00:02.5 – 00:02.7:** The whip-pan down from the top of the tower to the low-angle street view has a jarring hitch in the middle of the blur.
    *   **00:05.0 – 00:05.9:** The vertical camera slide down the facade into the white window is disorienting and ends in an ugly white-out (see Glitch #1).
    *   **00:09.4 – 00:10.0:** The cut from the swaying tower to the ruler fails as a match cut because the ruler's initial framing is completely abstract and unrecognizable until **00:10.2**.

### 4. Audio & Sound Design
*   **Voiceover:** The narration is crisp, well-paced, and sits clearly on top of the mix throughout.
*   **SFX Issues:**
    *   **00:01.7:** Good thunder crack on the first lightning bolt.
    *   **00:06.0:** The deep bass drop on the damper reveal is effective.
    *   **00:09.5:** **Missing SFX.** When the ruler is flicked on the desk, there is no "twang" or vibration sound effect—just the continuous storm bed. A ruler twang here is essential to sell the visual metaphor.
    *   **00:11.4 – 00:13.3:** The storm/rain bed stays at full exterior volume while we are inside the office looking at the water glass. Low-passing (muffling) the storm audio indoors would add huge polish.
    *   **00:27.5 – 00:30.7:** As the narrator discusses the hydraulic pistons turning motion into heat, there is a faint mechanical sound, but it lacks a distinct hydraulic hiss or thermal hum as the cylinders glow red.

---

### 5. Captions
*   **00:00.0 – 00:01.6:** The first caption (`In August 2015,`) appears at **00:00.2**, which is good, but it stays static until **00:01.6** while the narrator is already saying *"a super typhoon..."* at **00:01.1**. Tighten the change to **00:01.1**.
*   **00:11.3 – 00:11.6:** The caption `Up top,` flashes on screen for only ~0.3 seconds during a fast camera pan, making it barely readable. Combine it with the next line: `Up top, people start to feel seasick.`
*   **Accuracy & Placement:** Spelling (British English `metre`, `tonne`) is consistent, and the bottom-center placement never collides with key visual elements (the "1 m" floor marker at **00:33.5** sits safely above the text).

---

### 6. Factual Accuracy
*   **Accurate:** August 2015 (Typhoon Soudelor), 1-meter swing (record 100cm sway), floors 87–92, 660-tonne (728 US tons) steel sphere, 8 hydraulic viscous dampers dissipating kinetic energy as heat, and ~40% sway reduction are all factually spot-on.
*   **Visual Exaggeration Note:** At **00:07.6 – 00:09.4** and **00:30.8 – 00:32.7**, the tower is animated bending like rubber by what looks like 30–50 meters. While acceptable as a stylized visual exaggeration to demonstrate the concept on a small phone screen, it borders on looking comical.

### 7. Overall Instagram Performance & Scores
Yes, this will hold attention well on Reels. The hook is immediate, the monochrome indigo/gold palette is striking, and the pacing never lets the viewer get bored.

*   **Hook:** 9/10 (Strong visual storm + specific historical event + immediate lightning strike at 1.7s).
*   **Visuals:** 7.5/10 (Great style and palette, held back by the blurry cup shader, ruler clipping, and wind-direction error).
*   **Pacing:** 9/10 (Relentless camera movement and smart 2–3 second shot lengths).
*   **Audio:** 7.5/10 (Great VO, good storm ambiance, but missing key foley on the ruler and indoor audio ducking).
