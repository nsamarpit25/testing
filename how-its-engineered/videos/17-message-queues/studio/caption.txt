You hit buy on Black Friday. The order service calls payment directly, payment slows down, orders pile up, and the whole app goes down. 💥

The fix: put a message queue in the middle.

📨 Order drops your order in the queue and instantly says "received"
⚙️ Payment becomes a worker, pulling orders out at its own pace
📈 Traffic spikes? The line just grows. Nothing crashes
➕ Still too slow? Add more workers on the same queue
🔁 A worker dies mid-order? The queue only deletes an order once a worker says done, so it goes right back in line

A message queue lets one service hand off work without waiting for the next one. RabbitMQ and Amazon SQS are the popular ones.
