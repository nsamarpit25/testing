# Ep 7 facts (checked 2026-10-24)
Sources: Rijkswaterstaat, Maeslantkering page (RWS): https://www.rijkswaterstaat.nl/water/waterbeheer/bescherming-tegen-het-water/waterkeringen/deltawerken/maeslantkering ;
Watersnoodmuseum (WSM): https://www.watersnoodmuseum.nl/en/water-knowledge/learn-about-water-safety/articles/maeslant-barrier ;
Wikipedia (WP): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maeslantkering

1. Line 1. **3 m.** The barrier closes automatically when the water at Rotterdam is forecast to reach 3 m above NAP (Normaal Amsterdams Peil, roughly mean sea level) (RWS: "bij een waterpeil van 3 m boven NAP bij Rotterdam"). "Push the sea three metres higher" = a 3 m surge above normal. It closes the Nieuwe Waterweg, Rotterdam's waterway to the North Sea, with two gates (RWS, WSM).
2. Line 2. **210 m.** Two doors, each 210 m long, 22 m high and 15 m deep (RWS: "twee deuren van 210 m breed, 22 m hoog en 15 m diep"; WSM: 210 m long). They close the full 360 m width of the waterway (WP).
3. Line 3. **Dry docks.** When open, the gates rest in dry docks on the banks, which keeps them out of the water to prevent rust and allow maintenance (WSM; WP).
4. Line 4. **Docks flood, hollow doors float.** About 30 min before closing the dry docks are flooded and the gates start to float (WP). The gates are hollow pontoons, brought in empty (WSM).
5. Line 5. **Motors, ball joints, 10 m.** Motors on the shore (WP: "locomobiles") rotate the gates into the water (WSM). Each gate is anchored to the shore by a ball hinge 10 m in diameter weighing 680 tonnes (RWS: "bolscharnieren met een doorsnede van 10 m en een gewicht van 680 ton"; WSM). Each gate is held by a 237 m steel truss arm (WP).
6. Line 6. **Fill and sink.** When the gates are about 1.5 m apart in the middle, water is let into the hollows and they sink onto a prepared bed on the bottom (WP). RWS: "Bij sluiting lopen ze vol met water, waardoor ze binnen twee uur naar de bodem zakken."
7. Line 7. **Holds back the North Sea.** Designed for a surge up to 5 m above NAP (RWS: "kan een vloedgolf van 5 m boven NAP aan"); protects about two million people in South Holland (RWS).
8. Line 8. **Pumped empty, float home.** "To reopen, the gates are pumped empty and rotated back. They then float into a dry dock" (WSM).
9. On-screen only (optional): first automatic storm closure at the 3 m level on 21 December 2023 (RWS; WP); built 1991-1997.
10. Not used: the "as long as the Eiffel Tower is tall" comparison (sources disagree: arm vs whole barrier, 237 m vs 300 m).
