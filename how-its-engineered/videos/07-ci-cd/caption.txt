One typo shouldn't be able to take your whole app down. 💥

Big teams push code all day and their apps stay up. The difference? Nobody ships by hand.

Every change rides an assembly line:
🏗️ Build: a robot builds your app from scratch on a clean server, not your laptop
🧪 Test: every test runs automatically. One fails, the line stops
🚀 Ship: everything passed? It deploys on its own, often to a few users first, then everyone

That's CI/CD. CI is the checking. CD is the shipping.
GitHub Actions sets it all up with one file in your repo.
