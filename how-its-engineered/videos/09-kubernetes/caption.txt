Your app crashed at 3 AM. By the time you woke up, it was already back. Nobody touched it. 😴

With Docker alone, a crashed container stays dead until you restart it by hand. Kubernetes is the robot that does it for you: you say what you want, it keeps it that way. Like a thermostat.

🔄 Update: new version rolls out one copy at a time, zero downtime
