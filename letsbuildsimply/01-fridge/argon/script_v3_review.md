* **Beat 12 vs. 13 (Condenser location):** You show an exposed coil on the *back* (Beat 12), then tell the viewer to touch the warm *side* (Beat 13). Modern fridges have warm sides precisely because the condenser coils are hidden inside the side walls, not on the back. **Fix:** Say, *"On yours, these pipes are hidden inside the walls—go touch the side of your fridge."*
