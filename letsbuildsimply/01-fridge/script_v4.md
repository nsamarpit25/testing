This version was built with Argon in two passes: a draft with 8 hook options and a full scene plan, then a hard critique. I fact-checked and tightened it after that. It runs 272 words, about 88 s with Puck. There is no mascot; "you" is the builder.

Color rules:
- Heat is glowing orange orbs.
- Cold refrigerant is electric blue.
- Hot squeezed gas is red-orange.

## Facts
- Fridge refrigerants boil below freezing: R-600a at −11.7 °C, R-134a at −26.3 °C (at 1 atm).
- Evaporation absorbs latent heat (the shower shiver).
- Compression raises the gas temperature above room temperature, so heat flows out at the condenser, where the gas condenses while giving off heat.
- Modern fridges hide the condenser in the side walls, which is why the sides feel warm.
- The capillary tube bore is 0.5–1.5 mm (dry spaghetti is about 1.8 mm). The pressure drop makes the liquid flash-boil and go well below 0 °C.
- The thermostat holds the fridge just above freezing, about 3–4 °C.
- Freezing milk expands about 9%, which can crack a glass bottle.
- A fridge is a heat pump that moves heat from inside to the kitchen.

## What we kept against Argon's critique, and why
- **"This is the ___":** we kept it. It's the proven format of both references ("This is a blast valve").
- **Interactive lines:** we kept two of them, the shower question and "ever noticed…", because the creator asked for interactivity. We dropped the "lick your hand" instruction because Argon judged nobody would actually do it mid-scroll.
- **The defrost beat:** we cut it. It saves 10 s and Argon flagged it as a slow beat.
