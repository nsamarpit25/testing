What if you drilled straight through the Earth... and jumped in?
First, the drill. It rips through the crust like nothing.
But the deeper you go, the hotter it gets.
The mantle isn't lava. It's solid rock, glowing hot, under crushing pressure. Your drill starts to fail.
Below that? A sea of liquid iron... around a solid core as hot as the Sun's surface.
Your drill? Gone.
But suppose the tunnel existed. No air. No friction.
You jump. You fall faster... and faster.
At the center, there's no net pull... but you're at top speed.
You fly straight past, slowing down... until you just reach the other side.
Then you fall back. Without friction? Back and forth... forever.
