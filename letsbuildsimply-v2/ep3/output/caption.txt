What if you drilled straight through the Earth… and jumped in? 🌍

The mantle isn't lava. It's solid rock, glowing hot. Below it: a sea of liquid iron around a solid core about as hot as the Sun's surface.
In an ideal tunnel with no air and no friction, you'd hit about 28,000 km/h at the centre and stop right at the other side, about 42 minutes later. Then fall back. Forever.

Would you jump? 👇
