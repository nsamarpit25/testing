7. **The Meeting That Could Have Been An Email** — Claude joins her 1-hour meeting as notetaker; the whole summary is one sentence. Twist: the action item assigned to Cleo is "schedule a follow-up meeting".
8. **Undo Button** — she runs `rm -rf` on the wrong folder, panics, Claude calmly restores it from a backup it made "last time". Twist: "Last time" was this exact folder, on this exact day, last year.
9. **Claude Writes My Dating Profile** — "Make me sound interesting." It lists only true facts and they're devastating ("Owns 9 unused spinach bags"). Twist: first match is a guy whose profile Claude also wrote, same phrasing (series callback).

Strongest bets: 1, 5, 9 (agent chaos + series callbacks), then 3 and 6. Series picks (2, 10) build returning viewers.
