SLOT A

TITLE: Your Screenshots Have Rent

HOOK: “Claude, my screenshots are charging me rent.”

WHY IT SPREADS:
Feeling: Collecting advice feels like making progress.
Who tags whom: Anyone with a “useful someday” folder tags their fellow screenshot hoarder.

FULL SCRIPT — approximately 38 seconds

1. CLEO: Claude, my screenshots are charging me rent.
[Open on Cleo staring at her monitor. No opening text; subtitles enter after she starts speaking.]

2. CLAUDE: Your cloud storage is full.

3. CLEO: Explain cloud storage like I’m five.

4. CLAUDE: A cupboard in someone else’s house.
[Whiteboard cut: a little cupboard containing photo icons and a folder labelled “SAVE MONEY.”]

5. CLEO: Someone has my pictures?

6. CLAUDE: Your files live on computers elsewhere. You reach them through the internet.

7. CLEO: My phone’s just the cupboard door?

8. CLAUDE: One door. Your laptop opens it too.
[Whiteboard cut: phone and laptop connected to the same cupboard. Its doors bulge.]

9. CLEO: Fine. What’s taking up the space?

10. CLAUDE: Twelve thousand screenshots about saving money.
[Reveal at approximately 28 seconds: monitor shows “SAVE MONEY — 12,000 images.”]

11. CLEO: Those are going to make me rich.

12. CLAUDE: They’ve already earned someone a monthly payment.

13. CLEO: Explain a different word.

14. CLAUDE: Which word next? I suggest “savings.”

FINAL FRAME:
Cleo’s frozen expression beside “SAVE MONEY — 12,000 images” and a “Storage payment due” notification. Claude’s final subtitle remains visible.

CAPTION IDEA: The savings folder has become a monthly expense.
COMMENT BAIT: Which word should Claude explain like you’re five next?

SELF-SCORE:
Hook: 9/10
Retention: 8/10
Twist: 8/10
Shareability: 9/10


SLOT B

TITLE: Please Hold for Finance

HOOK: “Claude, the client wants our entire team.”

WHY IT SPREADS:
Feeling: The panic of looking bigger than you are.
Who tags whom: Freelancers tag the friend who says “we” while working alone.

FULL SCRIPT — approximately 36 seconds

1. CLEO: Claude, the client wants our entire team.
[Open on Cleo leaning toward Claude. No opening text; subtitles enter after she starts speaking.]

2. CLAUDE: All of me?

3. CLEO: I promised departments. They have twelve people.

4. CLAUDE: We have tabs.
[Monitor: DESIGN / FINANCE / LEGAL. Each tab contains a Claude conversation.]

5. CLEO: You’re design. Show them the logo.

6. CLAUDE: Done. Switching to finance.
[The active tab changes. Claude makes one tiny, officious adjustment to his posture.]

7. CLEO: Tell them we need a deposit.

8. CLAUDE: And legal?

9. CLEO: Say it again, but frightening.

10. CLAUDE: Payment is required before work begins.

11. CLEO: They want to reschedule. Their team’s unavailable.
[Client email arrives: “Sorry—our whole team is unavailable until 9.”]

12. CLAUDE: All twelve people?

13. CLEO: “We’ve reached our message limit.”
[At approximately 27–28 seconds, Cleo expands the email. The quoted sentence appears beneath the first.]

14. CLAUDE: This meeting could have been two prompts.
[Claude calmly closes the LEGAL tab.]

FINAL FRAME:
The client’s message beside Cleo’s three Claude tabs. Large final subtitle: “This meeting could have been two prompts.”

CAPTION IDEA: From our entire team to your entire team.
COMMENT BAIT: How many departments are you personally pretending to be?

SELF-SCORE:
Hook: 9/10
Retention: 9/10
Twist: 8/10
Shareability: 9/10


SLOT C

TITLE: Six Hours to Save Ten Seconds

HOOK: “Claude, I spent six hours saving time.”

WHY IT SPREADS:
Feeling: Buying the feeling of productivity instead of doing the task.
Who tags whom: Anyone who finishes work tags the friend still choosing a productivity app.

FULL SCRIPT — approximately 37 seconds

1. CLEO: Claude, I spent six hours saving time.
[Open on a visibly pleased Cleo. No opening text; subtitles enter after she starts speaking.]

2. CLAUDE: You automated your to-do list.

3. CLEO: Ten seconds saved. Every single morning.

4. CLAUDE: You’ll break even in about six years.

5. CLEO: That’s an investment.

6. CLAUDE: Assuming you keep using this app.

7. CLEO: Obviously. It’s my whole system.
[Monitor: an elaborate task dashboard. First task: “Stop wasting time.”]

8. CLAUDE: Shall I add tomorrow’s tasks?

9. CLEO: Actually, this other app has prettier checkboxes.
[Cleo switches tabs. The new app has the same tasks, with round checkboxes.]

10. CLAUDE: Does it run your automation?

11. CLEO: No. I moved everything already.
[Reveal at approximately 28 seconds. Claude looks from the abandoned dashboard to Cleo.]

12. CLAUDE: Including “stop wasting time”?

13. CLEO: Yes. Can you rebuild it?

14. CLAUDE: Of course. Restarting your six-year countdown.
[Claude brightens and turns toward the monitor. Cleo nods as though this is excellent news.]

FINAL FRAME:
The new app’s unchecked “Stop wasting time” task. Beside it: “TIME INVESTED: 6 HOURS / TIME SAVED SO FAR: 0.”

CAPTION IDEA: Spent the whole day improving how I’ll work tomorrow.
COMMENT BAIT: What task have you spent longer automating than actually doing?

SELF-SCORE:
Hook: 9/10
Retention: 9/10
Twist: 8/10
Shareability: 9/10
