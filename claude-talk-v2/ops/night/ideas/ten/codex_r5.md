RANKING:
1. STEVE THE FERN — The plant becoming a self-made influencer is the most distinctive, immediately shareable comic world.
2. MOM READ OUR CHATS — Family guilt plus private AI conversations reaches the broadest audience.
3. LIVE RÉSUMÉ — Public exposure creates immediate tension, and the recruiter joining the joke supplies a satisfying reversal.
4. JESSICA — Competing with the mechanism designed to addict you gives viewers a specific friend to tag.
5. SOMETHING CAME UP — Identical excuses are recognizable; money curing every emergency provides the missing payoff.
6. THE COMPLAINT — Appliances quoting their owner's review is a strong visual premise, weakened by the current betrayal ending.
7. WI-FI BURGLAR — Accidental trespassing is accessible, but the explanation currently gives away the reversal too soon.
8. TINY FAVOR — The audience recognizes the behavior immediately, including the supposedly surprising culprit.
9. PER MY LAST MESSAGE — Corporate phrases are familiar territory; Cleo involuntarily reproducing them is its strongest available distinction.
10. BLIND TEST — The joke depends on turning the show's loyal deadpan character into a smug hypocrite.
