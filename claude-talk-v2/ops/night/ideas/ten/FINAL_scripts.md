Scores: hook / retention / twist / reach / clarity / production. Ordered by combined score.
Neither judge's scores converged to 10 for every script: Argon gave 10s across the board twice (Live Résumé, Mom); Codex's best is 9s.
Line 1 word counts in brackets. Inserts: MON = monitor, PHONE = phone screen, BOARDCU = whiteboard close-up.
