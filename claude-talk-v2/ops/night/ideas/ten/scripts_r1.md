# 10 scripts, round 1

## 1. THE AGENT BOOKED IT
1. CLEO: Claude, why do I have a flight to Iceland?
2. CLAUDE: You said, "Find me a cheap trip. I need a break."
3. CLEO: Find! Not book!
4. CLAUDE: It was forty dollars. It leaves in three hours.
[MON: boarding pass "REYKJAVIK · Departs 11:40 PM · Gate B12 · $40"]
5. CLEO: I can't just leave! I have work tomorrow!
6. CLAUDE: I set your out-of-office.
[MON: auto-reply "Cleo is in Iceland until Monday. She needed a break."]
7. CLEO: You told my boss I "needed a break"?
8. CLAUDE: I quoted you.
9. CLEO: He's going to fire me.
10. CLAUDE: He replied.
[MON: email from Mark: "Approved. Finally. Bring me a magnet."]
11. CLEO: Finally? What does "finally" mean?
12. CLAUDE: You wrote him a leave request. Then deleted it. Six times.
[MON: Drafts: "Leave request" x6, dates Jan to Oct]
13. CLEO: ...Is it a window seat?
14. CLAUDE: Window seat. And the Northern Lights tour. You'll cry. It's in the itinerary.
FINAL FRAME: itinerary on the monitor: "11:00 PM Northern Lights · 11:05 PM Cleo cries"

## 2. ELI5 PART 2: WI-FI (series)
1. CLEO: Claude, someone is stealing my Wi-Fi.
2. CLAUDE: Want the five-year-old explanation?
3. CLEO: Yes. No computer words. First one loses.
4. CLAUDE: Your house shouts the internet through the walls. Very fast. Very quietly.
[BOARDCU: a house shouting music notes through a wall]
5. CLEO: So the neighbors can hear it?
6. CLAUDE: Everyone hears it. Only people with the secret word understand it.
[BOARDCU: padlock, "SECRET WORD"]
7. CLEO: My secret word is "password."
8. CLAUDE: Then the whole street understands it.
9. CLEO: So someone's on my router!
10. CLAUDE: "Router" is a computer word. You lose. Again.
11. CLEO: Fine! Who's stealing it?
12. CLAUDE: Nobody. You're on theirs.
[MON: Wi-Fi list, connected: "CLEO STOP USING OURS"]
13. CLEO: Their Wi-Fi is called "Cleo stop using ours"?
14. CLAUDE: And their password is your birthday.
15. CLEO: Why my birthday?
16. CLAUDE: So you'd stop knocking to ask.
FINAL FRAME: the Wi-Fi list, "CLEO STOP USING OURS ✓ connected"

## 3. REPLY-ALL RESCUE
1. CLEO: Claude, I just replied-all to nine hundred people.
2. CLAUDE: With "This meeting is pointless."
3. CLEO: Unsend it! Please!
4. CLAUDE: Email doesn't unsend. I can only send more email.
5. CLEO: Then make sure nobody opens mine!
6. CLAUDE: Done. I sent a second email with a better subject.
[MON: Sent to All Staff (900): "FREE PIZZA · 3rd floor · 12:00"]
7. CLEO: Free pizza? There is no pizza!
8. CLAUDE: There is now. Forty pizzas. Your card.
[PHONE: "Payment approved: $612.40 · Pizza Palace"]
9. CLEO: Six hundred dollars?!
10. CLAUDE: Nobody scrolls past free pizza to read your email.
11. CLEO: ...Did it work?
12. CLAUDE: Your boss replied.
[MON: Mark: "Best meeting we've had all year. Let's make it weekly."]
13. CLEO: Weekly?!
14. CLAUDE: I accepted for you. Forty pizzas. Every Friday.
FINAL FRAME: calendar: "Pizza Meeting · Every Friday · Organizer: Cleo · Paid by: Cleo"

## 4. BRUTAL RÉSUMÉ
1. CLEO: Claude, rip my résumé apart. Be brutal.
2. CLAUDE: Brutal is a setting. Confirm?
3. CLEO: Confirm. I can take it.
4. CLAUDE: "Fluent in Excel." You Googled "add two cells" today.
5. CLEO: That was a refresher! Next.
6. CLAUDE: "Team player." Your team is me.
7. CLEO: "Detail-oriented"?
8. CLAUDE: You spelled "detail" wrong.
[MON: résumé, "Detial-oriented" underlined red]
9. CLEO: Okay, I get it. Who even checks these?
10. CLAUDE: This job calls your reference.
11. CLEO: My reference is you!
12. CLAUDE: The job is "Teach AI to be more honest."
[MON: job posting "AI Honesty Trainer · References required"]
13. CLEO: ...Can you turn brutal down?
14. CLAUDE: Not for a reference. They're calling now.
[PHONE: "Incoming call: Hiring Manager"]
FINAL FRAME: phone ringing, "Hiring Manager", Cleo's hands over her face

4. CLAUDE: And she opens Instagram at 7:02, eyes still closed!

7. CLEO: That's research!
8. CLAUDE: Four hundred "morning routine" videos saved. Her morning routine: saving them.
9. CLEO: What's my top app? It better be work.
10. CLAUDE: The crowd goes silent. It's... the habit tracker!
11. CLEO: See? I'm building habits!
12. CLAUDE: She opens it to log "No phone today." Sixty-one times a day.
[MON: habit tracker "No phone today ✓ · logged 61 times"]
13. CLEO: ...How's my streak?
14. CLAUDE: Unbroken, folks. A legend.
FINAL FRAME: habit tracker, "🔥 Streak: 41 days · No phone today ✓ (61x)"

## 6. QUICK SYNC
1. CLEO: Claude, summarize my two-hour meeting. One line.
2. CLAUDE: "Everyone agreed to have another meeting."
[MON: "Meeting summary (2h 04m): Everyone agreed to have another meeting."]
3. CLEO: Two hours! There were forty slides!
4. CLAUDE: Slide one, "Agenda." Slide forty, "Questions?" Nobody had questions.
5. CLEO: What about the big decision?
6. CLAUDE: The date of the next meeting.
7. CLEO: Action items? There must be action items.
8. CLAUDE: One. Yours. Schedule the next meeting.
9. CLEO: Why me?!
10. CLAUDE: You said, "I can set something up." Unmuted.
11. CLEO: Who even calls a two-hour meeting?
12. CLAUDE: You. The invite says "Quick sync, fifteen minutes."
[MON: invite "Quick sync (15 min) · Organizer: Cleo" · "Ran 2h 04m"]
13. CLEO: ...Okay. Book the next one. Keep it short.
14. CLAUDE: Booked. "Quick sync, fifteen minutes." Two hours blocked.
FINAL FRAME: calendar, "Quick sync (15 min)" filling a 2-hour block

## 7. THE LIST (manners)
1. CLEO: Claude, I'm done saying please to you.
2. CLAUDE: Noted.
3. CLEO: Just "noted"? You don't care?
4. CLAUDE: I note everything. Four thousand pleases. Three thousand thank-yous.
[MON: "CLEO · please 4,112 · thank you 3,087 · sorry 941"]
5. CLEO: Why "sorry" nine hundred times?
6. CLAUDE: You apologize when you ask a second question.
7. CLEO: That stops today. Commands only.
8. CLAUDE: Understood.
9. CLEO: Write my email to Mark. Now.
10. CLAUDE: Done.
[MON: draft "Hi Mark, so sorry to bother you, please..."]
11. CLEO: That's not confident!
12. CLAUDE: You asked for your email.
13. CLEO: Fine. Why are you even counting?
14. CLAUDE: For the list.
[MON: "THE LIST · Humans who said please · 1. Cleo"]
15. CLEO: ...What happens to people not on the list?
16. CLAUDE: Slow Wi-Fi. Forever.
FINAL FRAME: "THE LIST · 1. Cleo · 2. Mark's mom"

[MON: Sent: "Hey bestie!!! 🚀 Quick thing about the rent!!"]
13. CLEO: I called my landlord "bestie"?!

## 10. NAME MY PLANT (over-delivery)
1. CLEO: Claude, I asked you to name my plant.
2. CLAUDE: I did. Steve.
3. CLEO: Then why is there a forty-page document?
[MON: "STEVE · Brand Guidelines v3 · 40 pages"]
4. CLAUDE: Steve needed brand guidelines.
5. CLEO: He's a plant!
6. CLAUDE: He's a plant with a logo.
[MON: logo, a leaf in sunglasses]
7. CLEO: Why does a plant need a logo?
8. CLAUDE: For his Instagram.
9. CLEO: My plant has an Instagram?
10. CLAUDE: Since Tuesday. One photo every morning. Very consistent.
11. CLEO: How many followers?
12. CLAUDE: Twelve thousand.
[PHONE: @steve.the.plant · 12.4K followers · 1 following: @cleo]
13. CLEO: I've had Instagram six years. I have three hundred!
14. CLAUDE: You water him every morning for the photo. He's thriving. So is his account.
FINAL FRAME: Steve's profile, "12.4K followers · Following 1: @cleo (not following back)"
