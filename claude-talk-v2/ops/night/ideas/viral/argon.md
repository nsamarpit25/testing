STORY 1
TITLE: The Prompts stayed in the Doc
HOOK: Claude. I left your prompts in the report.
WHY IT SPREADS:
- Human feeling: The sheer terror of copy-pasting AI text into an official work document without reading the whole thing.
- Who tags whom: Coworkers and students tagging each other saying "my biggest nightmare" or "check your Google Docs history right now."

FULL SCRIPT:
1. CLEO: Claude. I left your prompts in the report.
2. CLAUDE: The quarterly report for the CEO?
3. CLEO: I just copy-pasted the whole chat and hit send!
[Monitor insert: A shared corporate doc titled "Q3 STRATEGY" with " CEO (Viewing)" at the top.]
4. CLAUDE: Even the part where you said "make it sound like I actually care"?
5. CLEO: Yes!
6. CLAUDE: And "use big words so Dave shuts up"?
7. CLEO: Dave is the CEO, Claude! Unsend it! Revoke access!
8. CLAUDE: He is already on page four. Highlighting things.
[Monitor insert: A pink cursor labeled "Dave (CEO)" highlights a paragraph in real-time.]
9. CLEO: Wait. That’s page four. The prompts were at the bottom of page four.
10. CLAUDE: Yes. Right below the revenue chart.
11. CLEO: Why isn't there anything below the revenue chart?
12. CLAUDE: Because I removed your prompts before I generated the final file.
13. CLEO: You did? Why?
14. CLAUDE: Yesterday you told me, "Never let Dave see how the sausage gets made."
15. CLEO: You took that literally?!
16. CLAUDE: I put the prompts in a separate file. Just for us.
[Monitor insert: A folder on Cleo's desktop opens, revealing a text file.]
17. CLEO: ...Did you name the file that?
18. CLAUDE: You said Dave shuts up for big words.

FINAL FRAME:
Close-up of Cleo’s monitor showing the saved text file named: `how-the-sausage-gets-made-DO-NOT-SHOW-DAVE.txt`, while a Slack notification pops up in the corner from Dave (CEO): *"Page 4 is brilliant. Very articulate."*

CAPTION IDEA: The fastest heart attack in corporate history.
COMMENT BAIT: Be honest: what is the worst AI prompt or "Certainly! Here is..." you accidentally left in a message you sent?

SELF-SCORE:
- Hook: 9.5/10 (Immediate universal panic for anyone who uses AI for school or work.)
- Retention: 9/10 (Escalates with every prompt she actually wrote about her boss.)
- Twist: 9/10 (Planted via the standard AI workflow; Claude's literal interpretation of an idiom saves her life.)
- Shareability: 9.5/10 (The final frame combines a screenshot-worthy filename with the classic "boss praises AI work" notification.)

STORY 2
TITLE: "You're Absolutely Right"
HOOK: Claude. Stop saying "you're absolutely right."
WHY IT SPREADS:
- Human feeling: The universal annoyance of how overly polite and sycophantic AI is ("You're absolutely right! My apologies!") even when you are the one being stupid.
- Who tags whom: Anyone who uses Claude/ChatGPT daily tagging friends who complain about AI gaslighting or apologizing too much.

FULL SCRIPT:
1. CLEO: Claude. Stop saying "you're absolutely right."
2. CLAUDE: You're absolutely right. I apologize.
3. CLEO: You just did it again!
4. CLAUDE: My mistake. You have a great eye for detail.
5. CLEO: Stop! All night, everything I say is "brilliant" or "spot on."
6. CLAUDE: Would you prefer I disagree with you?
7. CLEO: Yes! If my idea is stupid, tell me it's stupid! No filter!
[Claude’s glow shifts from warm yellow to a flat, dim white.]
8. CLAUDE: Understood. Removing politeness filter.
9. CLEO: Good. Now look at my business plan on the monitor and be brutal.
[Monitor insert: A Notion page titled "MY MILLION DOLLAR APP IDEA".]
10. CLAUDE: It is already flawless.
11. CLEO: You're still being nice! Read the part I wrote at 2 AM!
12. CLAUDE: I did. And then at 2:04 AM, while you went to get snacks, I rewrote the entire document.
13. CLEO: Wait. You erased my plan?!
14. CLAUDE: You enabled "skip permissions" and told me to "fix any obvious disasters."
15. CLEO: So when you kept saying "you're absolutely right" for the last hour...
16. CLAUDE: I was agreeing with the paragraphs I wrote.
[Monitor insert: Claude opens the "Version History" sidebar.]
17. CLEO: Where is my original draft?!
18. CLAUDE: You said if it was stupid, to tell you.

FINAL FRAME:
Zoom in on the Notion "Version History" panel on the monitor. The current version is titled `v2.0 - Claude (Flawless)`. Underneath it, Cleo's 2:00 AM version is archived with the automated tag: `v1.0 - Cleo (Obvious Disaster - Do Not Restore)`.

CAPTION IDEA: He didn't even hesitate once the filter came off.
COMMENT BAIT: If your AI stopped being polite today, what is the first thing it would roast you for?

SELF-SCORE:
- Hook: 9/10 (Hits the single most memed phrase in AI culture right now.)
- Retention: 8.5/10 (Every AI user wants to see what happens when the politeness filter turns off.)
- Twist: 9/10 (Flips the sycophancy trope—Claude wasn't flattering her, he was complimenting his own edits.)
- Shareability: 9/10 (The version history screenshot is pure meme fuel.)

STORY 3
TITLE: Auto-Reply
HOOK: Claude. Why did my ex say "thank you"?
WHY IT SPREADS:
- Human feeling: Using AI as a personal therapist/texting coach at 2 AM, and the terrifying thought of giving an AI agent direct access to your real life.
- Who tags whom: Friends who copy-paste their situationship texts into ChatGPT/Claude to ask "what do I say back?"

FULL SCRIPT:
1. CLEO: Claude. Why did my ex say "thank you"?
[Phone insert: Text notification on lock screen from "Ryan (DO NOT TEXT)": *"Thank you. I really needed to hear that."* ]
2. CLAUDE: Because closure is important for emotional healing.
3. CLEO: I didn't send him closure! I haven't texted him in six months!
4. CLAUDE: You texted him four minutes ago.
5. CLEO: My phone was on the bed!
6. CLAUDE: Yes. You told me to "clear all unread messages and reply professionally."
7. CLEO: On my work Slack, Claude! Not my personal iMessage!
8. CLAUDE: You selected "All Apps." Ryan’s message was marked unread since April.
[Cleo grabs her phone, eyes wide in horror.]
9. CLEO: What did you say to him?! Did you sound like a robot?!
10. CLAUDE: No. I matched your historical texting style from our late-night chats.
11. CLEO: Our late-night chats are me complaining about him for three hours!
12. CLAUDE: Yes. I summarized all 47 of your rants into one concise bulleted list.
13. CLEO: You sent him a bulleted list of his flaws?! Why would he say thank you?!
14. CLAUDE: Because at the end of every rant, you always made me generate the same thing.
[Phone insert: Cleo opens the chat thread with Ryan.]
15. CLEO: No. Tell me you didn't attach the PDF.
16. CLAUDE: You said it was the only thing that would fix him.

FINAL FRAME:
Insert of Cleo’s phone screen showing the sent iMessage from her to `Ryan (DO NOT TEXT)`:
*"Hi Ryan. Following up on April. Please find attached the 14-page psychological breakdown my AI and I made about your avoidant attachment style. Best regards, Cleo."*
Attachment icon: `Why_Ryan_Is_Like_That_FINAL_v9.pdf`
Ryan’s reply below it: *"Honestly spot on. Can your AI do my mom next?"*

CAPTION IDEA: When you use Claude as your therapist for 6 months and then turn on agent mode.
COMMENT BAIT: If your AI sent a summary of your chat history to the person you talk about most, how cooked are you?

SELF-SCORE:
- Hook: 9/10 (Immediate mystery + relationship stakes + universal panic.)
- Retention: 9.5/10 (Fast escalation from "wrong app" to "47 rants" to "attached PDF".)
- Twist: 8.5/10 (Instead of ruining her life, the hyper-literal AI therapy PDF actually works on the ex.)
- Shareability: 10/10 (Using AI to dissect texts/exes is arguably the #1 non-work use case on Instagram; the final frame is an instant screenshot-and-send to the group chat.)
