**“Nothing New” — approximately 50 seconds, vertical**

Cleo sits at her desk, glasses catching the monitor’s light. Her editor is open. Keyboard and mouse sit untouched. Claude stands beside the keyboard. Her phone lies flat between them.

Claude’s movements are limited to small tilts, turns and bounces.

**0:00–0:07**

Close on the phone. A chat named **Maya**. Cleo has typed:

**I miss you.**

Her finger hovers over Send.

**CLEO:** How do I say this without sounding pathetic?

Claude leans toward the screen.

**CLAUDE:** Who’s it for?

**CLEO:** My best friend.

**CLAUDE:** That should help.

**0:07–0:15**

Cleo almost smiles. Her finger retreats.

**CLEO:** We haven’t talked in eight months.

Claude straightens.

**CLAUDE:** Did you fight?

**CLEO:** No.

A beat.

**CLAUDE:** What happened?

**0:15–0:29**

Cleo looks at the monitor.

**CLEO:** She got a job. Moved into her own place.

She nudges the mouse. The screen wakes fully, revealing the same unfinished project.

**CLEO:** Every time she asked what was new…

She looks around her room.

**CLEO:** I was still here.

Claude turns from the monitor to her.

**CLEO:** I kept thinking I’d reply when I had something good to tell her.

**0:29–0:39**

Close on Cleo. She rubs a fingerprint off her glasses with her sleeve, then puts them back on.

**CLEO:** Now it’s been so long.

She looks down at the message.

**CLEO:** What if she thinks I’m a mess?

Claude tilts toward her. His delivery is gently matter-of-fact.

**CLAUDE:** Cleo. She’s met you.

Cleo lets out one surprised little laugh.

**CLEO:** Fair.

**0:39–0:45**

She presses **Send**.

The message appears in the conversation.

Cleo pulls her hand back and sits very still. Claude stays beside her.

Two quiet seconds. Only the computer fan.

Then the phone vibrates.

**MAYA — INCOMING CALL**

**0:45–0:50**

Cleo looks at Claude, startled.

He gives one tiny, encouraging bounce.

She answers and taps speakerphone, leaving the phone on the desk.

**CLEO:** Hey.

Her shoulders finally drop.

**CLEO:** I missed you.

Cut on her small, relieved smile, before we hear the other end.
