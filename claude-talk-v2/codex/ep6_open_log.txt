1. KEEPING COMPANY
While Cleo builds, Claude asks if he’s helping—he’s been sitting quietly for ages. She says her mum used to sew beside her while she did homework; she never understood why her mum liked that so much.
Final line — Cleo: “I think I get it now.”

2. THE INVITATION
Cleo opens a ridiculous unfinished game she started with a friend she’s drifted away from. She spends longer writing the project invitation than fixing the game, eventually deleting everything except “Miss making stupid things with you.”
Final line — Cleo: “What if we just hang out and never finish it?”

3. THE PINK HEADPHONES
Her first freelance payment has bought a pair of bright pink headphones, which look comically out of place beside her serious setup. When Claude asks why those, she admits she wanted them at thirteen but told her mum she didn’t like them after seeing the price.
Final line — Cleo: “Turns out I still wanted the pink ones.”

4. ONE MORE MINUTE
The shelves are empty and moving boxes surround Cleo’s desk; she and Claude finish one last build in the room where she learned to code. She shuts the laptop, then opens it again just enough to light the familiar marks on the wall.
Final line — Cleo: “One more minute. Then we’ll go.”

5. THE USELESS BUTTON
Cleo spends an evening making a button that produces a tiny, spectacularly stupid trumpet noise. She laughs, then admits she can’t remember the last time she made something without wondering whether it would help her get hired.
Final line — Cleo: “Can we make it do another stupid noise?”

6. ALL GOOD
A rejection email sits behind Cleo’s editor while she replies “All good!” to a friend asking how the interview went. Claude watches her erase the reply; she finally types, “Actually, can I call you?”
Final line — Cleo: “I don’t want to do the cheerful version tonight.”

7. KEEP HER VOICE
Cleo finds a childhood game with a terrible microphone recording of her younger self enthusiastically announcing the title. While rebuilding it with Claude, she cleans up the graphics and replaces the broken controls, but stops when they reach the audio.
Final line — Cleo: “Keep that bit. She sounds so happy.”

8. SOMETHING OF YOUR OWN
Claude notices Cleo smiling at her phone while their build runs. Her younger sister has sent a picture of her first crooked little webpage, followed by “Don’t fix it, I just wanted to show you”; Cleo puts down her mouse.
Final line — Cleo: “She used to bring me drawings like this.”

Strongest: KEEP HER VOICE. Hearing her younger self’s unguarded excitement gives the emotion something concrete to land on, and “Keep that bit” makes a small editing decision feel like an act of tenderness.
ep6_short_reply.md
1. **“You Can Close the Laptop”**  
   Cleo keeps inventing tiny tasks because stopping makes her feel guilty. Claude closes his own miniature cardboard laptop and waits beside hers.  
   **Emotional turn:** “I finished an hour ago. I was waiting for you.”

2. **“The First Person I Told”**  
   Cleo gets her first paid project. She excitedly turns toward an empty room, then tells Claude every detail. Later, he asks why she hasn’t told anyone else.  
   **Her answer:** “With you, I didn’t have to pretend it was bigger.”

3. **“Just Dinner”**  
   Cleo dismisses a message from her mum asking what she ate. She wishes her family understood her work. Claude notices every message ends with the same question.  
   **Emotional turn:** They don’t understand the code. They’re trying to look after the person writing it.

4. **“The Interview Smile”**  
   After a difficult video interview, Cleo keeps smiling while describing how badly it went. Claude quietly asks whether the camera is still on.  
   **Ending:** She checks. It isn’t. Her smile finally falls.

5. **“Nobody Clicked”**  
   Cleo launches something she spent months building. The visitor count stays at one. She asks Claude whether that visitor is him. He shakes his head.  
   **Emotional turn:** It’s her—checking again, hoping someone came.

6. **“The Embarrassing Folder”**  
   Cleo finds her first website and wants to delete it. Claude asks her to open it once. A crooked button still works.  
   **Ending:** She remembers how excited she was the first time it did, and quietly keeps the folder.

7. **“Same Desk, Different Lives”**  
   Cleo scrolls through friends’ promotions, weddings, and new homes, then looks around her unchanged room. Claude points to an old height mark scratched into the desk.  
   **Emotional turn:** She’s been measuring everyone else’s milestones because hers don’t photograph well.

8. **“Don’t Fix It Yet”**  
   Cleo begins describing a terrible day. Claude immediately reaches for a little troubleshooting checklist. She gently puts it down.  
   **Key line:** “Can you be upset with me for a minute?”  
   He sits beside her. The checklist stays untouched.

9. **“The Empty Evening”**  
   A project ends. For weeks, Cleo wanted free time; now the quiet makes her restless. She opens her laptop again.  
   **Ending:** Claude slides their unfinished doodle between the screen and keyboard. She remembers something she wanted to do without needing to finish it.

10. **“For Tomorrow’s Cleo”**  
    Exhausted, Cleo leaves a harsh note for herself about everything she failed to finish. Claude asks her to read it as though tomorrow’s Cleo is already sitting there.  
    **Ending:** She rewrites the first line: “Start here. I’ve done the difficult setup for you.”
