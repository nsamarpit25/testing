**1. “Try to Break It”**  
Cleo complains that every app she builds has exactly one tester: her friend, who sends ridiculous bug reports. Tonight, another arrives: “Button doesn’t work when I click it with a biscuit.” Claude asks how long they’ve been doing this. She scrolls back. Years.  
**The turn:** She finally messages: “You always show up.”

**2. “Who Wrote This Garbage?”**  
Cleo and Claude roast an awful piece of code before discovering it’s her first project. In the README: “Got the button working today!!!” She quietly stops laughing.  
**The ending:** She runs it. One ugly button appears. She clicks it, and smiles like it’s working for the first time.

**3. “Can We Not Fix It Yet?”**  
A project Cleo spent weeks on gets rejected. Claude immediately starts suggesting improvements. She snaps: “Can you let it be sad for a minute?” He goes quiet. A long beat.  
**The ending:** “A whole minute?” he asks. She almost laughs. “Maybe two.” He settles beside her keyboard.

**4. “Sorry. Stupid Question.”**  
Cleo keeps apologizing while asking Claude something basic. Their usual bickering starts, until he asks why she whispers the questions. She admits someone once laughed at her for asking one.  
**The ending:** He asks, simply, “From the beginning?” She nods. This time, she asks at full volume.

**5. “One User”**  
Cleo calls her app a failure: one active user. Claude notices that person returns every morning. It’s a tiny gardening tracker she built for her mum. Today’s entry includes a crooked photo: “First tomato!”  
**The ending:** Cleo starts enlarging the photo button her mum keeps missing.

**6. “The Message Without a Send Button”**  
Cleo keeps asking Claude to rewrite a message to an old friend: warmer, shorter, less needy, more casual. Eventually, he asks what she actually wants to say. “I miss you.”  
**The ending:** She deletes the polished paragraph and types those three words. After sending, she immediately complains about how exposed she feels.

**7. “The Missing Square”**  
Cleo is devastated that she broke her coding streak. Claude helps investigate the missing day as though they’re debugging a crime. Then she opens her calendar: it was the evening her brother came over, and they talked until morning.  
**The ending:** “Oh,” she says. She closes the contribution graph.

**8. “Show Me”**  
Cleo finally launches her app. She refreshes the page, checks her messages, refreshes again. Nothing. Claude’s celebratory hopping slowly stops. Then he asks her to demonstrate it. “You helped build it.” “I haven’t seen you show it to someone.”  
**The ending:** She straightens up. “Okay. So, this is the home screen…”

**9. “You Made It Better”**  
Claude helps polish a birthday webpage Cleo made for a friend. It becomes beautiful—and she hates it. They argue until she admits the wonky drawings were hers, and the terrible animation was an inside joke.  
**The ending:** They restore it. Claude watches a lopsided cake wobble across the screen. “That is objectively dreadful.” “She’ll love it.”

**10. “The Comment”**  
Cleo nearly deletes an old tutorial because her explanation is clumsy and her code is embarrassing. Then she finds a recent comment: “Everyone else skipped this bit. Yours was the first one I understood.”  
**The ending:** She fixes one broken link and leaves the tutorial up. Somewhere, her beginner self is still helping someone.

My strongest picks: **#3** for their relationship, **#2** for a quiet visual ending, and **#1** for something viewers will send straight to the friend who keeps showing up.
