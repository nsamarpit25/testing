**“MAKE IT WORSE” — approximately 50 seconds, vertical**

Late evening. Cleo sits at her desk, glasses catching the monitor’s glow. Her keyboard and mouse sit in front of her; her phone lies beside them. Claude stands near the monitor.

No music at first. Just room tone and mouse clicks.

**0:00–0:05 — Close on Cleo.**

She’s trying to keep a straight face.

**CLEO:** I spent three hours making this.

Claude leans forward.

**CLAUDE:** Show me.

**0:05–0:11 — Over Cleo’s shoulder.**

On the monitor: a plain webpage with one enormous button marked **PRESS**.

She clicks.

**SOUND:** A grand trumpet fanfare that immediately collapses into a pathetic squeak.

Silence.

Claude looks from the screen to Cleo.

**CLAUDE:** Again.

She clicks. The same ridiculous noise.

Cleo snorts, surprising herself. Claude bobs with a small laugh.

**0:11–0:18 — Two-shot.**

Still smiling, Cleo moves the cursor toward the tab’s close button.

**CLEO:** Okay. Back to something useful.

Claude straightens.

**CLAUDE:** Wait. I wasn’t done.

**CLEO:** It only does that.

**CLAUDE:** I know.

Her cursor stops.

**0:18–0:25 — Close on Cleo, with Claude at the bottom of frame.**

She clicks once more.

**SOUND:** The fanfare fails again.

She laughs softly, then leaves her hand resting on the mouse.

**CLEO:** I used to make stuff like this all the time.

**CLAUDE:** Bad trumpets?

**CLEO:** Things nobody asked for.

**0:25–0:34 — Hold on their faces.**

Claude tilts toward her.

**CLAUDE:** Why’d you stop?

Cleo looks at another browser tab: **PORTFOLIO**.

A small pause.

**CLEO:** Started needing everything to go on my résumé.

Claude looks at the button. Then back at her.

Let the room stay quiet for a beat.

**0:34–0:40 — Close on Claude.**

He studies the screen with complete seriousness.

**CLAUDE:** Can you make it worse?

Cleo turns to him.

The smallest smile.

**CLEO:** Oh, definitely.

**0:40–0:45 — Quick jump cut.**

Cleo’s fingers are on the keyboard. Claude waits beside it, leaning toward the monitor.

A second button now sits beside the first.

Its label: **WORSE**.

She moves her hand to the mouse.

They exchange a look.

She clicks.

**0:45–0:50 — Two-shot. Hold through the reaction.**

**SOUND:** A much longer, deeper trumpet failure, ending with one tiny, delayed squeak.

Claude rocks backward.

Cleo breaks into a proper, unguarded laugh.

**CLEO:** That’s disgusting.

Claude waits until she looks at him.

**CLAUDE:** Keep it.

Still laughing, she clicks it again.

**Cut to black on the first terrible note.**
