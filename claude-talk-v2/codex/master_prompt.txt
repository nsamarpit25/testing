                        THE INSTAGRAM REEL
                       SCRIPT MASTER SYSTEM
    A repeatable process for turning a niche + raw idea into audience-led hooks, scripts,
                          CTAs, and retention-focused reel assets.

 The core principle: Don't start by asking AI to “write a script.” Start by giving it a
 problem to solve, an audience to serve, and a structure designed for attention.


 1. The Full Process
 Step 1 — Define the niche and target viewer
 Tell ChatGPT exactly who the content is for. “Fitness” is broad; “busy professionals trying to lose their
 first 5 kg” is actionable.

 Step 2 — Find the audience’s real problems
 Identify recurring pain points, frustrations, objections, mistakes, desires, fears, and questions. Prioritize
 problems that are frequent, emotionally relevant, and useful for short-form content.

 Step 3 — Choose one problem for one reel
 Do not cram five lessons into one reel. Pick one core problem and make the entire reel feel like a
 solution to that problem.

 Step 4 — Turn the problem into a promise
 The viewer should immediately understand what they will get. The promise creates a reason to keep
 watching.

 Step 5 — Build Hook → Value → Solution → CTA
 The hook earns attention. The value proves the viewer is in the right place. The solution gives them a
 practical takeaway. The CTA tells them what to do next.

 Step 6 — Create two hooks: verbal + text
 The verbal hook is what you say. The text hook is what appears on screen. They should work together
 rather than simply repeat each other.

 Step 7 — Engineer retention
 Use curiosity, open loops, specificity, contrast, pattern interrupts, short sentences, and progression.
 Remove slow introductions and filler.

 Step 8 — Write a CTA for the goal
 A follower CTA should give a reason to follow. A comment CTA should make responding easy. A
 save/share CTA should connect to the value.

 Step 9 — Package the reel
 Generate on-screen text, B-roll/visual suggestions, delivery notes, caption, cover text, and a final
 quality check.

 3. How to Use It
 • Minimum input: niche + target audience + raw script/idea.

 • Best input: add reel goal, duration, tone, and offer/product if relevant.

 • Don't polish the raw script first. A messy idea is useful because the prompt is designed to diagnose
 and rebuild it.

 • One problem per reel. If several problems emerge, turn them into separate reels.

 • Record the final version. Use the visual plan to keep the video moving.

 • Keep your voice. Change any line you would not naturally say.


 4. The Strategy Behind Your Original Reel
 Your idea works because it teaches creators to stop treating AI like a “script vending machine.” The
 stronger positioning is: <b>AI can write the words, but the creator needs a content strategy first.</b>

 • Audience → Who are we talking to?

 • Problem → What are they struggling with?

 • Angle → What is the most useful way to frame it?

 • Hook → Why should they stop?

 • Value → Why should they stay?

 • Solution → What can they do differently?

 • CTA → What should they do next?

 • Packaging → How do we make it easy to watch, understand, and act on?


 5. Suggested CTA for Your Reel
 <b>Recommended:</b> “If you want the exact master prompt that finds your
 audience’s problems, builds the hook, writes the script, creates the CTA, and gives you
 the full reel plan—comment <i>REEL</i> and I’ll show you the full process.”

 If your delivery method is a link, DM, freebie, or downloadable resource, swap the final action. The key is to sell the
 <b>outcome</b>, not merely the existence of a prompt.


 6. Quick Creator Checklist
 ☐ I know exactly who this reel is for.

 ☐ I identified one painful/desirable problem.

 ☐ The hook is specific, not generic.

 ☐ The viewer gets value before the CTA.

 ☐ The solution is actionable.

 ☐ The verbal and text hooks complement each other.

 ☐ The reel has a reason to keep watching.

 ☐ The CTA matches the reel goal.

 ☐ The script sounds like something I would actually say.

 ☐ Every sentence earns its place.
